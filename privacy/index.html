Privacy Policy

Search Center for iPhone and iPad

HomeSupport

Effective 23 November 2026

In short

Search Center collects nothing. The developer runs no server and never receives your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your list of search sites and your settings are stored on your device, in Safari's storage for the extension. They are not sent to the developer or anyone else.

When you tap a search button

The text you selected is sent to the site you tapped (for example, a search engine or a shop), as part of that site's search address, and opens in a new Safari tab. From then on that site's own privacy policy applies. Nothing is sent until you tap a button; selecting text alone sends nothing.

Search site icons

To show each site's icon in the bar, the extension asks that site for its icon (its favicon), the same way Safari does when you visit it.

If a site has no usable icon, the extension asks DuckDuckGo's icon service for it, sending only the site's name (such as example.com), never the text you selected. You can turn this off in the extension's settings ("Use DuckDuckGo when a site's own icon is missing or blurry"). DuckDuckGo's privacy policy is at duckduckgo.com/privacy.

Backups

A backup file is created only when you choose Copy backup or Save as file, and it goes only where you put it. It contains your list of search sites and your settings.

The Search Center app

The app on your home screen shows setup help, whether the extension is working, and your list of search sites. To show the list, the extension shares it with the app on the same device: the sites' names, addresses and icons, the bar setting, and the time the bar last appeared. It never shares the text you select or the pages you visit, and none of this leaves your device. The app does not collect, send or share any data.

Children

Search Center collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact
